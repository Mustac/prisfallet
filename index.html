Annonse. All shop links on Kupp.no are affiliate links (annonselenker). If you buy or click through them, Kupp.no may get a commission from the shop or the affiliate network. This never changes the price you pay.

Prices, product names and photos come from the official product feeds that the shops publish through affiliate networks (Adtraction, Awin, Daisycon, Kelkoo). Photos are shown straight from the shop or network, not copied. Updated once a day.

How we show discounts. "Førpris (butikkens oppgitte)" is the before-price the shop gives in its feed. "Laveste pris siste 30 dager" is the lowest price Kupp.no itself has recorded for the product over the last 30 days. We only use the second label when we really have 30 days of our own price history. Only products in stock and at least 30% off are listed. Not affiliated with any shop.
